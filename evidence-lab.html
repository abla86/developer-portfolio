RESEARCH, BUT PLAYABLE.

A deliberately small public demonstration of structured evidence review. It is independent from the private Evidence Appraisal Tool and does not expose its code, data, methodology library or full research workflow.

MINI REVIEW · 01

Evidence signal

Work through the questions. This demo produces a transparent review signal, not a validated methodological quality score.

1. Is the research question clearly defined?
2. Is the study population relevant to the question?
3. Can the primary outcome be located in the source?
4. Are important limitations reported?
5. Can the finding be independently traced to the source?

Answer the five questions.
